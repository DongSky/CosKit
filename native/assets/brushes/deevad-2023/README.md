# David Revoy — January 2023 brush tips

Author: David Revoy (Deevad). Asset license: CC0-1.0 (the article itself has a different license).
Source: https://www.davidrevoy.com/article953/krita-brushes-2023-01-bundle
Download: https://www.peppercarrot.com/extras/resources/deevad-bundle_23-01.zip
The bundle meta.xml explicitly declares CC-0. The article explicitly permits redistribution and inclusion in software.

19 original tip files are retained here with SHA-256 in manifest.json. CosKit embeds converted grayscale masks, maximum 192 px on the longest side, derived with scripts/import-deevad-brushes.py. Animated GIH tips use their first frame and are labelled accordingly. These are adapted native sampled brushes, not emulation of Krita paint/mixing engines or a claim to preserve all 38 Krita presets.

The 9 CosKit cosplay presets (hair, soft light, fabric, dust, stars) are original parameter designs under the project licenses. The drawing made with these brushes remains the user's work.
